What the app is

Hadees Vijnanakosham is a Malayalam–Arabic hadith encyclopedia. You can read, search, bookmark and ask questions about the hadith in it. You do not need to create an account.

What stays on your device

  • Bookmarks and notes, recently read hadith, recent searches, and display settings (font, size, alignment).
  • This data is stored only on your phone or browser. We cannot see it and it is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

What is sent to our servers

  • Reading and searching: the app fetches hadith text from our database (hosted on Supabase). Search words you type are sent to it so it can return matches. We do not link these requests to you.
  • "Ask AI" and "By meaning" search: the question you type is sent to our Cloudflare Worker, which uses Cloudflare Workers AI to find relevant hadith and write an answer. Answers may be cached so identical questions are served faster. Do not type personal information into questions.
  • Feedback: only if you press Send in the Feedback screen. We store your message, the type you chose, the screen or hadith it refers to, your platform (iOS, Android or web), and your contact detail only if you choose to enter one. The contact field is optional.
  • Abuse protection: to limit spam, the feedback service temporarily counts requests per network address for up to one hour. The address is not stored with your feedback.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no user profiles.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or device identifiers.
  • We do not sell or share your data.

Service providers

The app runs on Supabase (database) and Cloudflare (hosting, Workers, Workers AI). They process requests on our behalf under their own privacy terms.

Retention and deletion

Feedback is kept until we have read and acted on it. To ask for your feedback message to be deleted, send a new message through the Feedback screen quoting what you wrote, with a contact detail so we can reply.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.
